Katie’s preview process

The little details
only she would notice.

When Katie S. received the first embroidery preview of her orange cat, she could immediately see what needed to change.

The overall portrait was there, but the fur, eyes, and expression still needed to feel more like the cat she knew by heart.

1. The first embroidery preview

We placed K.S.’s original photo beside the first embroidery preview so she could compare the expression, colors, features, and overall likeness.

Original photo

First embroidery preview

2. Adjusting her coloring

K.S. told us that her cat’s fur should be a darker orange and her eyes should be light green. We updated both colors and sent a new image for review.

“It looks much more like her!”

Feedback from K.S.

3. Refining the mouth

The mouth still felt slightly different, so K.S. asked us to make the line thinner and a little longer. After she selected the final artwork, we used that approved design to create the finished embroidery.

Final approved artwork

Finished embroidered piece

“I think the second design is my favorite :)”

Final selection from K.S.
