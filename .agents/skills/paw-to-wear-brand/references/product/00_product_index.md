Do not load all product files for every task.

Examples:
- product detail / email about a garment → garment + relevant pattern + pricing/policy only if needed.
- customization copy → relevant pattern + customization.
- process transparency campaign → process + policy.
- offer copy → pricing + separate dated campaign truth (future module).
