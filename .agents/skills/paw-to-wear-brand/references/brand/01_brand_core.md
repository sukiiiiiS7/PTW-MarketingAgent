# Brand Core

**Status: CANONICAL for current brand definition**  
**Primary source: Current image-based Paw to Wear Brand Book**

## Brand

**Paw to Wear**

**Brand idea / line:**  
**Wear What Matters.**

## Positioning Statement

> Paw to Wear is a pet-inspired lifestyle fashion brand that transforms custom embroidery into wearable storytelling, celebrating the deep emotional identity and daily joy of pet ownership.

## Brand Promise

> Every stitch tells a story.  
> Every story deserves to be worn.

## Mission

> To create elevated everyday fashion inspired by the bond people share with their pets.

## Vision

> To become the leading lifestyle fashion brand for modern pet lovers.

## The Line of Connection

> The stitch is not decoration.  
> The stitch is symbolism.

Every line represents:
- memory
- companionship
- connection
- presence

---

## Strategic context from the Brand White Paper

The White Paper supports the same category direction: Paw to Wear should grow beyond a custom embroidered apparel store into a pet-inspired lifestyle fashion brand, and the long-term opportunity is emotional identity, celebration of pet ownership, and wearable storytelling.

It also frames the brand as helping people wear, celebrate, and remember the companions that shape their lives.

### Brand architecture

- Lifestyle — 60%
- Celebration — 30%
- Honor — 10%

Strategic role:
- Lifestyle drives growth.
- Celebration drives seasonal revenue.
- Honor creates emotional depth and long-term loyalty.

## Agent framing rule

Default to framing Paw to Wear as:
- pet-inspired lifestyle fashion
- wearable storytelling
- an expression of the bond between a person and a specific pet
- part of everyday life, identity, celebration, and memory

Do **not** reduce the brand by default to:
- a personalized gift shop
- generic pet merchandise
- a custom sweatshirt store
- novelty personalization

SEO or channel requirements may use category/search terms such as “custom pet sweatshirt,” but those terms should describe the product, not redefine the brand.
