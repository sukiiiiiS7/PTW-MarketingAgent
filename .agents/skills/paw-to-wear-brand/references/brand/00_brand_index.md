# Brand Knowledge Index

## Canonical brand files

1. `01_brand_core.md` — core identity, positioning, promise, mission, vision.
2. `02_brand_voice.md` — canonical keywords plus derived writing behavior.
3. `03_audience.md` — audience hierarchy and lifestyle context.
4. `04_content_system.md` — shared content pillars and content taxonomy.
5. `05_visual_identity.md` — color and typography system.
6. `06_brand_expression_rules.md` — canonical expression examples, hard language rules, CTA rules, promotion tone, memorial guidance, and the fast brand-quality checker.

## Retrieval guidance

For most marketing writing tasks, retrieve:
- `01_brand_core.md`
- `06_brand_expression_rules.md`
- only the relevant parts of `02_brand_voice.md`
- relevant audience/lifestyle from `03_audience.md`

Retrieve `04_content_system.md` for campaign/content planning and `05_visual_identity.md` for visual, email HTML, landing-page, image-generation, and design tasks.

Do not load every brand file by default. Prefer the smallest set that can safely answer the task.
