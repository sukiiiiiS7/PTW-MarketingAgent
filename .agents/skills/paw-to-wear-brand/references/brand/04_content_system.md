# Content System

## Canonical Content Pillars

### 1. Lifestyle
**How you live in it.**

Role:
Show how Paw to Wear naturally lives inside modern pet-parent routines and style.

### 2. Stories
**The meaning behind the stitch.**

Role:
Reveal the specific relationship, memory, ritual, personality, or reason behind a piece.

### 3. Community
**Real stories from real customers.**

Role:
Build belonging, proof, participation, and customer-led storytelling.

### 4. Fashion
**Styling. Details. Inspiration.**

Role:
Protect the “fashion first / lifestyle fashion” positioning through styling, garment detail, design, and aesthetic context.

### 5. Pets
**The heart of every story.**

Role:
Keep the specific pet at the emotional center without turning the brand into generic pet content.

## Shared taxonomy for future channels

Every content asset may be tagged with:

```yaml
content_pillar:
  primary:
  secondary:

brand_architecture:
  lifestyle | celebration | honor

lifestyle:
  coffee_walk | wellness | travel_companion | home_companion |
  dog_mom_club | celebration | outdoor | memory_honor

pattern:
  optional

story_type:
  everyday | gift_celebration | memorial_honor

funnel_stage:
  optional
```

This taxonomy should be shared by email, blog, SEO, Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube, paid social, and landing pages rather than creating a different content logic for every channel.
