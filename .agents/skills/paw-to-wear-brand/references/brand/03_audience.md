# Audience

## Strategic audience hierarchy

### Primary
Style-conscious pet parents, broadly 22–40.

### Secondary
Gift buyers.

### Tertiary
Memorial and remembrance buyers.

## Shared psychographic truth

Customers see pets as more than a separate “pet category.” Pets can be:
- companion
- family
- emotional support
- adventure buddy
- travel partner
- routine
- identity

The pet naturally appears throughout the customer’s lifestyle.

---

## Lifestyle system

The White Paper defines eight lifestyle territories:

1. Coffee Walk Lifestyle
2. Wellness Lifestyle
3. Travel Companion Lifestyle
4. Home Companion Lifestyle
5. Dog Mom Club Lifestyle
6. Celebration Lifestyle
7. Outdoor Lifestyle
8. Memory & Honor Lifestyle

### Priority lifestyle territories

The strategy recommends focusing first on:
- Coffee Walk — Everyday
- Dog Mom Club — Identity
- Travel Companion — Aspiration
- Home Companion — Lifestyle Expansion

## How an agent should use audience knowledge

Do not mechanically insert persona demographics into copy.

Use audience context to decide:
- which desire is active
- which everyday moment matters
- which identity signal matters
- whether the task is lifestyle, celebration, gifting, or honor
- which proof or product detail reduces friction

## Important distinction

“Memorial” is a meaningful brand territory, but it is not the default lens for all Paw to Wear communication.

Default growth communication should normally begin with lifestyle and everyday identity unless the task, product pattern, customer context, or campaign clearly indicates otherwise.
